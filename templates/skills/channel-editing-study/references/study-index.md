## Approved provisional design direction

The user approved this as the house style for all original videos (2026-11-02); it is
implemented as the `bytemonk` profile (see [house style](../../original-edit-planning/references/style-bytemonk.md)).
Keep it labeled as creative choices rather than a verified channel match:

- Reusable original dark-card/diagram vocabulary; clear heading/body/code
  hierarchy. Define accessible accents per project, with labels/icons. Topic
  colors and operational-state colors are separate, not a universal dictionary.
- Reveal causally meaningful nodes/edges/code fields with the narration. Use
  readable holds and restrained easing; no fixed "3-6 second" cutting formula.
- Charts need data/units/axes/sources; tables need readable rows and consistent
  comparison dimensions. Label synthetic output and conceptual schematics.
- Let short explainers and long labs pace differently. Show prerequisites,
  chapter orientation, command/result continuity and time-compression disclosures.
- Favor intelligible original narration, licensed ambient music and purposeful
  sparse UI cues. Exact audio processors, ducking method and loudness are unknown.
- Choose narrative structure by the teaching goal; keep source claims, assets
  and creator branding out of an original edit unless separately licensed/verified.

## New candidate production techniques

- Misconception/reality cards should label the contrast and use comparable visual
  weight. Do not invent a straw-man quotation or imply that a schematic is evidence.
- Source overlays should retain publisher, author/date and enough surrounding
  context for the highlighted passage to remain faithful. Use only material
  permitted for the project; prefer a cited paraphrase when reuse rights are absent.
- Animated charts require actual data, units, axis bounds, time range and source.
  Reveal the plotted path without changing its values; label illustrative charts.
- Equation/flow particles can motivate a concept but are not a validated fluid
  simulation, proof of a mathematical result or evidence of model capability.
- Ring layouts, pipeline steps and a comparison slider are optional explanatory
  layouts. Portraits/branding require appropriate rights and must not imply
  endorsement or personal attribution unsupported by sources.
